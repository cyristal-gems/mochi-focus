# Mochi Focus — Jamendo Lofi Collections

Six non-overlapping collections of nine Jamendo tracks each. Selections are based on explicit lofi titles and catalog metadata, not an audio listening review. These are Mochi playlists, not Jamendo-operated radio stations. Verified against Jamendo API v3 on September 25, 2026.

The app fetches current streaming URLs and license information by track ID. Unavailable tracks are skipped; broad genre searches and third-party streams are never substituted. Each collection repeats after its last track.
